Not sure what you're looking at?

Here's what to look for:

Water Chestnut

Floating rosettes of triangular, serrated leaves. Forms dense mats on the water surface.

Water Chestnut (Seed)

Hard, spiky black or brown seed pods, often washed up along shorelines.

Water Hyacinth

Thick, glossy round leaves with inflated stems. Purple flower spikes.

Hydrilla

Long, submerged stems with small pointed leaves in whorls of 4-8.
